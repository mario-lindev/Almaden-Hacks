# Gold and food sponsor logo audit

Audited November 9, 2026. Existing genuine brand assets were retained; no logo
was generated or reconstructed from a company name.

| Local asset | Official source and verification |
| --- | --- |
| `yri.png` | [YRI Fellowship](https://www.yriscience.com) identifies [this PNG](https://www.yriscience.com/yrilogo.png) as its brand logo in its structured data. The existing local file is byte-for-byte identical to that official image. It is a white-and-gold horizontal wordmark on a square black canvas. |
| `elevenlabs.svg` | [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) uses the matching two-bar symbol and full ElevenLabs wordmark as the inline SVG in its primary header. The existing vector is the complete logo, not a text replacement. |
| `losgatos.png` | [Los Gatos Meats & Smokehouse](https://www.losgatosmeats.com) directly links its [official header logo](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c180292620b85f549bb01e2/1545162207229-OFL0HQZHFNZLUU5XC7VH/lgmeats_logo.png?format=1500w). The local version is its black full logo on transparency; use a light tile. |
| `togos.png` | [TOGO’S](https://togos.com) uses [this PNG](https://togos.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/logo.png) in its primary header and organization structured data. The existing local asset is the full orange TOGO’S logo with its tagline. |
| `innout.svg` | Existing official In-N-Out 2021 vector logo, sourced earlier from the [official asset](https://www.in-n-out.com/ResourcePackages/INNOUT/content/images/logo-2021.svg) through its [Wikimedia archival copy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:InNOut_2021_logo.svg), as recorded in `SOURCES.md`. The official host blocked automated re-fetching during this audit; the genuine existing vector was preserved. |

Logos remain the property of their respective owners. This audit does not change
sponsorship status; pending food partnerships must remain visibly marked pending.
