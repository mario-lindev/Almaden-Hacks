# Original event illustrations

Generated specifically for Almaden Hacks with the built-in image generation tool on November 9, 2026. These are illustrations, not photographs of a past event.

- `almaden-scene.webp`: wide Almaden Valley landscape with three boys building at a picnic table.
- `almaden-mobile.webp`: portrait adaptation preserving the complete group on mobile.

Art direction: textured gouache and oil pastel, cornflower blue sky, gold meadows, blue Santa Cruz Mountain foothills, California live oak, warm afternoon sun. No text is baked into the art.
