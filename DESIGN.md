# Almaden Hacks

An illustrated event website for a student-run software and hardware hackathon in Almaden Valley. The design takes visual cues from tokensand.ai: a complete scenic hero, compact floating navigation, clear dark typography, spacious editorial sections, and warm illustrated colors.

## Identity

- Original painted Almaden Valley artwork: cornflower sky, blue foothills, golden grass and California oak.
- Three boys collaborate at a picnic table, small within the landscape.
- The hero and interface use Roc Grotesk, matching the Luma event. The compact navigation wordmark and footer retain Instrument Serif.
- Near-black text replaces the faded blue-gray text. Supporting text remains readable against warm ivory.
- No decorative eyebrow labels or heading scramble effects.

## Layout

1. Full scene with event title, registration and Discord actions.
2. Event date, location, time and live countdown.
3. About and a keyboard-accessible software/hardware/team panel with original live ASCII artwork.
4. Partners and confirmed sponsors, ordered by tier and card size.
5. Native FAQ disclosures and footer credits.
6. A separate Our Team page with the roster grouped by role.

The mobile hero has its own portrait composition so all three students remain visible. Navigation collapses to an accessible menu with Escape handling. Registration and Discord open directly in new tabs. There is no embedded Luma checkout.

## Assets

`public/art/almaden-scene-soft-sky.webp` and `almaden-mobile.webp` are original generated illustrations, optimized as WebP. The landscape appears only in the hero; the mobile version is an alternate composition, not a repeated section image.

Event: June 5, 2027, 8:00 AM PDT, Almaden Valley, San Jose. All existing event descriptions, FAQs, sponsor data, outbound links and footer credits remain intact. No venue or additional event commitments are invented.
